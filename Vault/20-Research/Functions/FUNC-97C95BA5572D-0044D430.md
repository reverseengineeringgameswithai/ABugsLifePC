# Function 0x0044D430

## Summary

Iterates records rooted at the globals established by `0x0044BB70`, performs
camera-relative center/bound tests, and builds candidate lists.

## Inferred

This is a scene visibility/update function. For the tested dispatch-`0x49`
records, the first three dwords are candidate-center components rather than the
render-placement tuple; exact axis names and units remain unknown. Positive
`+0x0C` is a visibility-bound candidate. Its negative-value convention and
other dispatch behavior remain unknown.
