# Function 0x00439E90

## Summary

Contains a literal-50 cap on grain counter `0x0059D9B1`, but its surrounding
role and applicability to Training remain unidentified.

## Confirmed

- `0x00439F05` loads literal 50 into `EBX`.
- `0x0043A0EB–0x0043A0F3` compare the byte at `0x0059D9B1` and write 50 when
  its unsigned value is higher.
- In an `EXP-20260814-112` 30-second breakpoint window after the controlled
  Training `49 -> 50` pickup, the cap write was not reached.
- The exact accepted Training patch leaves this function byte-identical to
  retail, while the in-level HUD and final tally retain/display 51.

## Inferred

This is probably a level- or mode-specific update with a counter cap. There is
not enough evidence to name the specific level, mode, or event.

## Unknown

- Call conditions and the exact game state that reaches the cap.
- Whether it is used by another level, a minigame, a reset path, or an
  unobserved Training mode.
- Semantics of the surrounding globals and branches.

## Corrected inference

`EXP-20260814-111` initially treated the static cap as a Training counter
boundary. `EXP-20260814-112` discriminates that attribution: the cap exists,
but it was not reached in the controlled Training transition and is not needed
for the accepted 51 result. Its Training identity is therefore unconfirmed.

## Later findings (2026-10-02 audit pointer)

- [[EXP-20260816-146 - Grain-gate level exclusivity static proof]] (checked
  independently by [[EXP-20260816-147 - Independent level-analysis audit]],
  check A2) partly answers the first two Unknown items: this is the routine
  for case 14 of the per-level dispatcher `0x00418780` (jump table
  `0x004187E8`, thunk `0x00401195`, call site `0x004187D2`). Internal index 14
  is Bug Bar in [[PC level roster and numbering]]; that name is
  researcher-provided. Training is internal 17 (`0x0043C340`), consistent
  with EXP-112 never reaching the cap.
- EXP-146 also records a grain-below-40 scripted event at `0x0043A911`. It
  is not a gate.
- EXP-20260930-004 lists this routine among
  the writers of 5 to jump state `0x0059D990`; that write's role is not
  established.
