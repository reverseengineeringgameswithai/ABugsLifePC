# Function 0x004129A0

## Summary

Renamed 2026-10-02 from `candidate_main_loop_and_mode_transition`: its
only callers are the video mapper's 40 calls through thunk `0x00401870`, and
it calls the DirectShow player (see Later findings). The original summary
follows.

Long-running candidate that reacts to a selected display-mode record, releases/recreates graphics resources, constructs a path, and processes Windows messages.

## Confirmed

- When a selected record changes, it releases clipper, Z-buffer, back-buffer, and front-buffer interface pointers. Evidence: `EXP-20260811-003`, decompiler at `0x004129A0`.
- One branch calls the DirectDraw2 `SetDisplayMode` vtable slot and then internal setup functions. Evidence: `EXP-20260811-003`, diagnostic string `0x004C60DC` and indirect call.
- References `pc\bugs\cd\` and `\cd\temp\` while constructing a local path. Evidence: `EXP-20260811-003`, strings `0x004E9071` and `0x004C60C8`.
- Calls `PeekMessageA`, `TranslateMessage`, and `DispatchMessageA`. Evidence: `EXP-20260811-003`, call references.

## Inferred

- This is a top-level loop or loop-adjacent controller that can apply display-mode changes.
  *Superseded 2026-10-02:* the caller evidence below places it under video
  playback; the mode change and message pump fit a video's display switch.
  Not yet observed at runtime.
- The path construction may select installed/CD content roots, but the controlling globals are not understood.
