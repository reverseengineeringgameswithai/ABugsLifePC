# Function 0x0041C6C0

## Summary

Coordinates several player-state outcomes from a caller-supplied fourth
argument. One branch supplies state bit `0x10` consumed by the candidate
type-1 nearest-row initializer. Collision/reset wording remains provisional.

## Confirmed

- The zero-argument arm includes a decrement of word `0x0059D9A4` and player
  reset/state writes.
- At `0x0041C8CC`, a nonzero fourth argument other than exactly 2 ORs
  `0x14` into state word `0x0059D9A0`.
- The exact-2 arm ORs only `0x04`.
- [[FUNC-97C95BA5572D-00432930]] separately gates its type-1 row scan on the
  resulting `0x10` bit.

## Inferred

The joined branches are consistent with distinct damage/reset outcomes, one
of which requests authored-row selection. Static evidence alone does not name
the gameplay event.

## Unknown

Meanings of all argument values, caller-specific event identities, the life
word's complete policy, and whether the path always leads to a visible
respawn.

## Dynamic evidence

  (EXP-20260930-006). Both call sites rechecked by disassembly of retail
  `97c95ba5…` on 2026-10-02.
