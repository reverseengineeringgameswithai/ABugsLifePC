# Function 0x004ACA60

## Summary

Height resolver over the cells collected by `0x004AC460`, reached through
thunk `0x00401717`. Selects one plane and writes its height plus `0xC80`
into the query block's Y.

## Confirmed

- Per cell: local coordinates `(x - base) >> 5` (arithmetic); the sign of
  word `+0x02` selects triangle winding; three `0x004A9290` half-plane
  tests over vertices `V0=(+0x06,+0x0A)`, `V1=V0+(+0x0C,+0x10)`,
  `V2=V0+(+0x12,+0x16)` decide containment.
- Contained plane height is exactly
  `base_y + 4*((+0x08 << 3) - idiv(((z-z0)*+0x04 + (x-x0)*+0x00) << 3, +0x02))`
  (`0x004ACC3E-0x004ACC7D`, toward-zero `idiv`).
- Planes with `h < y-0x600` compete for the maximum (tie: greater
  `+0x02`); others compete for the minimum, winding-1 only. The maximum
  wins only when its cell's `+0x02` is negative (`0x004ACD27-0x004ACD36`);
  otherwise the minimum; otherwise Y is set to the `0x80000000` failure
  sentinel.
- PC world Y grows downward: Level03 walkable ground rests near
  30000-32000 while the result-1 reset surfaces rest near 64032.

## Inferred

The maximum bucket acts as the resting-surface selection under a sentinel
query; winding-positive cells behave as one-sided surfaces.

## Unknown

Semantics of the mirrored result globals beyond `0x007F29A0/A4` and of
cell words `+0x0E/+0x14/+0x1C`.
