# Function 0x0041BD10

## Summary

Copies selected fields from the pointer retained at destination record
`+0x80` into one `0x84`-byte record. The structural copy is confirmed; field
meanings remain unidentified.

## Confirmed

- `0x0041BD19-0x0041BD24` reads source byte `+0x0C`, zero-extends it, and
  writes the destination first dword.
- `0x0041BD58-0x0041BD5C` copies source word `+0x12` to destination `+0x5C`.
- `0x0041BD60-0x0041BD65` copies source byte `+0x11` to destination word
  `+0x62`, subject to the later value-100 adjustment.
- `0x0041BDFB` writes zero to destination `+0x44`.
- Direct callers are `0x0041C559`, `0x0041ED3A`, `0x0043213E`, and
  `0x00436257`. The first three can reach fixed-pool slots 2 or 3; the last
  targets separate record `0x0059C9C0`.

actor, or field semantics are claimed.
