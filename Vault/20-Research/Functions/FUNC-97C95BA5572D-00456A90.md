# Function 0x00456A90

## Summary

Walks the relocated type-`0x3F` table and initializes the word at entry
`+0x0E` from a terrain query, with a distinct selector-`0x14` input path.

## Confirmed

- Reads pointer/count `0x005C595C` and advances entries by `0x10` at
  `0x00456A90–0x00456B4B`.
- Loads literal `0x14` at `0x00456AB2` and compares it with entry byte
  `+0x0C` at `0x00456ACC–0x00456AD1`.
- On that branch, forms a query from entry coordinate dwords, calls the terrain
  helper through thunk `0x00401721`, shifts its result by five, and stores the
  word at entry `+0x0E` (`0x00456AD3–0x00456AF5`).
- In `EXP-20260814-111`, the appended entry initializes to signed word `-1048`;
  adjacent originals initialize to `-1051` and `-1043`.
- In `EXP-20260814-113`, moved selectors `0x10`–`0x13` take the alternate
  non-`0x14` route. Their authored y dwords of `-50` initialize to
  `-1853/-1849/-1842/-1854`, with signed words `+0x0E` of
  `-1053/-1049/-1042/-1054`.

## Inferred

The word at `+0x0E` is a runtime terrain-height result for selector `0x14`.
The proposed function name is deliberately candidate-scoped because the exact
terrain helper semantics and other selectors' correction table remain
incomplete.

## Unknown

- The full contract of thunk `0x00401721` / implementation `0x004ABE00`.
- Why non-`0x14` entries use a distinct pre-query y input and correction table.
- The lifecycle and semantics of entry byte `+0x0D`.

## Later findings (2026-10-02 audit pointer)

- [[EXP-20260908-021 - Native collectibles and Blender placement]] (static,
  Unicorn runs in `tests/test_level_collectibles.py`, fresh live rows) answers
  the second unknown. Both branches store the queried floor at `+0x0E`. Grain
  rows (selector `0x14`) keep their literal Y. Other rows get
  Y = floor − `0x004C6A88[-sourceY/50]`: source offsets `0..-450` are height
  presets, not world heights (`0x00456B0B`–`0x00456B3A`, rechecked in this audit).
- Same experiment: `0x00456BA0` culls/scans the table and sets byte `+0x0D` to
  0/1. The rest of that byte's lifecycle is not traced here.
- Because grain Y is literal, grains authored below a floor stay buried
  (EXP-20260911-004); the level writers now
  place grains above their surface.
- [[EXP-20260927-001 - Complete original level Blender maps]] runs this routine
  offline over every original level's table to place pickups. Those heights
  were not compared with a live game (Known Unknowns).
