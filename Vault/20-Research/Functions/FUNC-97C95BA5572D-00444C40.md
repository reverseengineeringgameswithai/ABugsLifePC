# Function 0x00444C40

## Summary

Accepts a base path and numeric resource ID, requests a basename-matched
`.ANM`/`.ALL` pair when the corresponding caches are empty, and connects their
immediate consumers to shared scene-resource tables.

## Confirmed

- Appends `.anm` from `0x004DDC14` to the supplied base path and calls the
  whole-file loader at `0x00444CD8`.
- Advances the supplied arena cursor by the loader return rounded up to four
  bytes, stores the loaded pointer in per-ID table `0x005B18E0`, writes two
  header fields, and calls `0x00444B70` at `0x00444D21`.
- Passes the unchanged base path and the same cursor to `0x00448010` at
  `0x00444D69`; that helper constructs and consumes the `.all` request.
- For accepted Training `chars\flick`, resource ID zero selects ANM slot
  `0x005B18E0`. The `.ALL` consumer initially materializes 25 entries; this
  coordinator detects final entry word `+0x2E == 12` at
  `0x00444D97-0x00444DA7`, decrements the end index, and stores retained
  range `[0,24)` in the ID-zero header (`EXP-20260812-034`).
- Uses tables `0x005A9450` and `0x005A9250` as alternate already-loaded
  pointer paths. The current scene initialization clears both before the
  retained loader observations. Evidence: `0x004671D0`, `EXP-20260812-015`.
- Calls `0x00446970` at `0x00444E10` after the two immediate consumer paths.
- The exact pair path accepts plant 10 `.ANM` bytes at the plant 04 request
  while retaining plant 04 `.ALL`, completing Training initialization and
  reaching `NextLife` (`EXP-20260812-018`).
- The exact pair path also accepts plant 10 `.ANM` and `.ALL` bytes together
  at the plant 04 paths and reaches `NextLife` (`EXP-20260812-019`).
- A one-byte Training selector suppression removes both plant 10 request
  lines while preserving plant 04 and reaching `NextLife`; after diagnostic
  normalization those two deletions are the entire log difference
  (`EXP-20260812-020`).
- For live resource ID `0x71`, the per-ID ANM store at `0x00444D06` resolves
  to slot `0x005B1AA4`. `RUN-GDB-20260812-153150` measures its plant 10 value
  as `0x006C342B`; runtime header words `+0x04/+0x06 == 66/68` bound two
  global `0x50`-byte entries (`EXP-20260812-021`).
- Static instructions in its `0x00446970` callee read selected entry `+0x0C`
  behind `+0x2E` gates. Neither exact plant 10 `+0x0C` dword is read through
  resource-batch return or a no-input title (`EXP-20260812-025`).
- Neither exact plant 10 `+0x2E` word is read through the same guarded
  return/title window, so neither exact entry reaches those comparisons in the
  retained interval (`EXP-20260812-026`).
- Neither exact plant 10 `+0x28` word is read through the same guarded
  return/title window; both remain unsigned zero (`EXP-20260812-027`).

## Inferred

The function coordinates a related resource pair. The exact relationship and
whether either file represents a complete asset are unknown.

## Unknown

- Semantic names for the per-ID tables and header fields.
- Complete contract with `0x00446970`.
