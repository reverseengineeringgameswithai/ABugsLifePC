# EXP-20260812-014 - PC content census and loader context

## Question

What resource families exist in the exact derived PC game tree, and which
paths are explicitly loaded by unchanged Training and Level 01 runs?

## Inputs and procedure

- Source root:
  `/home/dev/ABLPC/work/copies/BUGS_PC-full-disc/PC/BUGS`.
- `scripts/census-pc-content.py` recursively records relative path, size,
  SHA-256, extension, and top-level content family. It emits no file payload.
- Root `bugs.log` is explicitly excluded because it is generated by research
  runs and does not occur in the disc listing.
- The 7-Zip disc listing contains 720 files below `PC/BUGS`. The remaining
  censused file is `bugs.exe`, copied from the disc root into the demonstrated
  fallback layout; both copies have executable SHA-256 `97c95ba5…7b878`.
- Loader context comes only from lines beginning `LOAD : Loading file` in the
  accepted hardware Training control `RUN-WINE-20260812-000634` and Level 01
  control `RUN-WINE-20260812-114514`.
- Paths are normalized case-insensitively below `PC/BUGS` and matched to the
  census. `Getting length` and type/setup messages are not counted as file
  loads.

No PS1 source was consulted.

## Census result

- Tree fingerprint SHA-256:
  `b145e2aa5fbf7e3350cf437985bbc76c764c36f2d954105d5282af4831738986`.
- 721 files totaling 460,306,509 bytes.
- 30 extension buckets and 26 path-family buckets.
- Largest extension populations: 344 `.wav`, 81 `.all`, 61 `.anm`, 47
  `.raw`, and 40 `.bug` files.
- Five duplicate-hash groups cover 26 files. Eighteen are empty placeholders;
  four non-empty pairs are recorded exactly in the JSON. Duplicate bytes do
  not prove identical runtime roles.

The complete metadata is `reports/content/pc-content-census.json`; its compact
extension/family view is `reports/content/pc-content-census.md`.

## Loader-context result

- Training log SHA-256 `cff5b275…04f8`: 25 unique explicit loaded paths,
  all matched.
- Level 01 log SHA-256 `5e7b93e7…386fa`: 45 unique explicit loaded paths,
  all matched.
- Their union contains 51 files and their intersection contains 19.
- The observed union comprises 22 `.all`, 18 `.anm`, seven `.raw`, three
  `.dat`, and one `.txt` file.
- Every explicit path matches the inventory; unmatched count is zero for both
  logs.

Static evidence independently ties the exact load-log text at `0x004E93AC` to
the whole-file loader candidate at `0x004AE240`. The logs therefore confirm
path requests through that function's message boundary, but they do not yet
identify each caller or downstream parser.

## Candidate family selection

`CD/PLANTS` is selected for the M2.2 loader trace as a **candidate subfamily**:

- 22 files totaling 82,432 bytes form 11 basename-matched `.ANM`/`.ALL`
  pairs;
- nine pairs are explicitly loaded in at least one retained scene;
- plant 04, 05, 06, and 10 pairs are loaded in both Training and Level 01;
- plant 02, 03, 07, 08, and 09 pairs are Level 01-only in these logs;
- plant 01 and 11 are not observed in either retained scene.

Pairing and common layout are not confirmed merely by names. M1 visibly
changes plant-like placements in Training, which makes this family useful, but
no M1 target-to-file dependency is asserted.

## Confirmed

- The census matches the derived-tree provenance described above and contains
  metadata only.
- All 70 per-log unique load observations resolve to their exact inventory
  files; the union contains 51 files.
- `CD/PLANTS` provides a bounded, repeatedly observed PC-native input set for
  caller/parser tracing.

## Inferred

- Repeated basename-matched `.ANM`/`.ALL` loads suggest a relationship worth
  testing. It is not yet evidence that the two extensions share a format or
  that each pair forms one complete resource.

## Unknown and limitations

- Caller return addresses and downstream consumers for the plant loads are not
  recorded.
- No plant file is linked to a specific M1 record or payload target.
- Logs record explicit whole-file load requests, not proof that every byte was
  processed successfully.
- Audio/SFX activity is not represented by these explicit load lines and may
  use another API/path.

## Result and follow-up

M2.1 is complete. M2.2 should map the PC callers and downstream consumers for
the shared `CD/PLANTS/PLANT{04,05,06,10}.{ANM,ALL}` loads, determine whether
the extensions have distinct loader paths, and retain all field/format names
as unknown until static or runtime evidence supports them.
