## Implemented contract

`reimplementation/ablpc/grain_gate_resolver.py` supplies two explicit request
types rather than a general family dispatcher:

1. `TunnelsRequest` requires internal level 3, signed int32 Flik X/Z,
   unsigned byte grain, an explicit radius, and exactly nine ordered
   `TunnelsRecord` values. Each record preserves type, address-named unknown
   `+0x04`, guarded `0..50` requirement, center X, and center Z.
2. `CityRequest` requires internal level 4 plus one `CityGate`: index 0..2,
   signed state, guarded requirement, center/radius, and both slots/deltas.
   `city_retail_requirement()` exposes the original `10*(index+1)` formula;
   configured thresholds can be supplied explicitly.

Outputs separate:

- per-gate eligibility and the first native non-opening reason;
- ordered direct writes with target address, width, value, and instruction;
- Tunnels private records after **direct opening writes only**, so coupling is
  visible without pretending decay ran;
- downstream rule metadata, including row 4's later same-pass decay entry after
  row-0 coupling, the City's 32-step zero-based aggregate deltas, and the fact
  that its first step occurs in the opening call.

Tunnels records changed only through coupling are not reported as independently
eligible. City index 0's second write is emitted as
`MemoryWrite(address=0x00599C69, width_bits=8, value=1,
instruction_address=0x00433807, target="address_0x00599C69")` — no semantic
field name.

wrong level/family, wrong record count, non-int/bool fields, out-of-int32
