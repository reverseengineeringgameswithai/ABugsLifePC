restored to the pristine derived tree.

Result
------
Bounded negative. This fixed trajectory produces no stable replacement-local
visual region in either control comparison. Retire the plant-04/10 direct
visible-outcome route. This does not prove that the files are unused,
semantically interchangeable, or invisible under every later event/camera.
