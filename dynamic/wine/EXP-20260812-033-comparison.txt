Control repeatability and direct measurements
---------------------------------------------
The unchanged controls are byte-identical at start samples 1 and 3:
  start/1 c2949393c6fef0ed71538fb5f7a298323fd50d87c4fb09db37c720359883cdb5
  start/3 04c0a4e7d4b610135633113219a5484c76131360d63cc7a147e940d83ddd4e08

Replacement-to-that-exact-control RMSE is 0.0311287 at start/1 and 0.0406163
at start/3. The later controls are not globally phase-identical: their
same-index RMSE ranges from 0.0508476 to 0.0720664 after movement. Global RMSE
therefore cannot by itself discriminate the substitution and is retained as
a limitation rather than normalized away.

Repeated local discriminator
----------------------------
For each anchor, every one of the three replacement samples was compared with
all six control samples (three from each fresh control): 18 absolute grayscale
difference masks. Their pixelwise intersection prevents any one control arm,
sample index, or animation phase from defining the result.

Pixels surviving all 18 comparisons at a 5% grayscale threshold:
  start:         689 pixels, 41x195+302+190, largest component 265 pixels
  after-up1:     466 pixels, 44x202+319+195, largest component 194 pixels
  after-right:    89 pixels, 20x64+341+230,  largest component 62 pixels
  after-up2:     532 pixels, 41x195+304+189, largest component 115 pixels

At 2%, the corresponding counts are 1,498, 1,667, 352, and 2,348 pixels;
each remains confined to the same moving local figure. At 10%, only one start
pixel survives and the other anchors are empty. This bounds the demonstrated
color/intensity delta rather than hiding the threshold dependence.

Visual inspection of the direct frames and the 18-way masks confirms that the
surviving regions lie on the input-controlled central on-screen figure at all
four trajectory anchors and move with it. No stable intersection pixel lies
outside the listed local boxes. Background, plant, ground, HUD, and camera-
phase residuals disappear under the 18-way intersection.

Every accepted arm followed a clean scoped Wine-prefix shutdown. The final R:
mapping was restored to the pristine derived tree, and no scoped process or
private mute sink remained.

Result
------
Positive bounded discriminator. The exact one-path ALL replacement is accepted
by the unchanged executable and causes a repeated, local rendered difference
on the input-controlled central figure along all four tested anchors. This
confirms a causal loaded-file-to-rendered-region link for the exact paths and
hashes in this experiment. It does not establish ALL layout or field semantics,
filename identity, general replacement compatibility, an M1 payload link, or
the complete character loader/runtime-object chain.
