Successful natural-pause watchpoint sequence
--------------------------------------------

Training was selected and allowed to stop at its natural "press a key to start"
screen. Native GDB was attached at that pause. A one-byte hardware read
watchpoint was installed on each record's first byte, one at a time; a full
dword read triggers each watchpoint.

  record 124: ESI=0x00659F37, EAX=0xFFFFFF5A, stop PC=0x0044D5BA
  record 125: ESI=0x00659F4B, EAX=0x0000034D, stop PC=0x0044D5BA
  record 126: ESI=0x00659F5F, EAX=0x00000368, stop PC=0x0044D5BA

Static disassembly of the exact executable bytes identifies the completed read:

  0x0044D5B8  mov eax,DWORD PTR [esi]
  0x0044D5BA  sub eax,edx

This address lies inside function 0x0044D430. After the last watchpoint was
removed, continuing and detaching allowed InitMainGame and NextLife to complete.

Negative debugger observations
------------------------------

1. A hardware read watchpoint installed only after gameplay began did not fire
   during 25 seconds that included movement input. This does not prove the
   records are never revisited; it shows that this late observation window did
   not include a read.
2. Native GDB warned that selected architecture i386:x86-64 was incompatible
   with the reported i386:x64-32 target. A software breakpoint at 0x00467976
   (the instruction after the 0x0044BB70 call) hit, but continuing immediately
   raised SIGSEGV at 0x00467976. Treat this as debugger interference. The
   hardware-watchpoint method at the natural game pause remained stable.
3. WineDbg's --gdb proxy injected a debug thread and later produced an unhandled
   fault at 0xFFBF0D6C in that injected thread. Treat this as tool interference,
   not a game defect; the target was restarted before the successful run.
